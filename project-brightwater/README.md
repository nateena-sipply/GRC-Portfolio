# Project Brightwater: An End-to-End GRC Case Study

> ⚠️ **Fictional organization; composite scenario; no real student, patient, or client data.** Educational material, not legal advice. All work is simulated.

## The Scenario
*Brightwater Intermediate Unit* (fictional) provides itinerant services to young children across 18 contracted sites. Staff include employees and independent contractors who often work from personal devices. The organization relies on a cloud student information system, assistive-technology vendors, and a new AI documentation tool it is piloting.

**Leadership's question:** *"Are we safe to scale the AI pilot and renew our core vendor after the sector's biggest vendor breach?"*
